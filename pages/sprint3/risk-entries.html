Three things that could sink the plan, and how we will know.

People keep using free social apps, Maps and AI instead of a planner

Trigger: Fewer than 2 of 5 new interviewees describe a planning failure costly enough to change habit.

Owner: Joy

Mitigation: Run follow-up interviews on last-time stories. Compare a prototype itinerary with what people do today.

Evidence: E-01, E-02, E-03, E-06

Our budget and total-cost feature has no direct demand evidence

Trigger: Fewer than 2 of the next 5 interviewees describe tracking or overspending trip costs as a problem. So far 0 of 3 mentioned budgeting.

Owner: Luis

Mitigation: Ask how they kept track of what the trip cost. Test Wanderlog's budget tool further.

Evidence: E-01 to E-05

Willingness to pay is unproven

Trigger: Fewer than 3 of 10 interviewees pay for any trip-planning tool, or a pricing test shows resistance.

Owner: Marc

Mitigation: Ask what they spent planning their last trip. Test a free tier with a paid upgrade.

Evidence: E-01 to E-04
