Interviews

Three travelers told us how they plan trips today.

Our 15-minute guide

  1. Screen (1 min)Do they use a competing solution now, or did they recently?
  2. Last-time story (4 min)Walk through the most recent time they did the job.
  3. Alternatives (3 min)What else they tried, and why it lost.
  4. Switching story (4 min)What pushed them to start, what almost stopped them, and what would make them leave.
  5. Money and time (2 min)What their current approach costs them.
  6. Close (1 min)Who else should we talk to?

What we heard

Three interviews, held together on Oct 13, 2026, with travelers from outside our course. Interviewees are coded P1 to P3.

P1: Peru trip

Booked flights early. Used TikTok, Booking.com, Priceline and ChatGPT. Pays about $20/month for AI.

P2: Seattle trip

Found places on TikTok and checked Google Maps reviews. Used ChatGPT. Spent nothing on planning.

P3: Albuquerque road trip

Planned around an event found on Instagram. Booked a hotel on Expedia. Says she needs a planner for her next trip.

Common thread: everyone used several apps for one trip, and nobody mentioned budgeting.
