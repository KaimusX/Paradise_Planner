Why people might switch

Ranked by evidence. Each rests on one or two of three interviewees.

  1. Trip coming up, no help planningP3 planned right after finding her event and wants a planner. (E-03)
  2. Saving timeP1 pays about $20/month for AI that saves time. (E-01)
  3. Keeping costs downP1 chose flights by price. P3 picked a hotel over Airbnb as cheaper. (E-01, E-03)
  4. Help when something goes wrongP1 got little help from booking sites. (E-01)
  5. Fewer appsAll three used several apps, but none called it a problem. (E-01 to E-03)
