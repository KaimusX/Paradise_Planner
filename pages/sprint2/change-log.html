Sprint 2 — Team 11

Sprint 2 Change Log

Changes from Sprint 1, their reasons, and their downstream impact.

No changes to the Sprint 1 documents

The Business Charter, Business Strategy, and Market Research were not revised. The entries below are clarifications and additions made while estimating and budgeting in Sprint 2.

CL-01 · Estimate and budget cover one slice

  • What changedSprint 2 estimates and budgets only the slice "Finding Events and Budgeting," not the full Release 1.
  • WhyThe estimation activity sizes a slice so two methods can be compared on a bounded scope.
  • ImpactExpected 555 hours (range 331–828). Budget request $136,197.26 for the slice, about $94,550 per year to operate. The full release is not yet priced. The Sprint 1 scope is unchanged.
  • Approved byProject Manager (full scope authority per the Sprint 1 charter)

CL-02 · Revenue model stated consistently

  • What changedThe charter named affiliate associations as the only funding. The strategy added a $1.99 purchase. Sprint 2 treats both as revenue.
  • WhyThe two Sprint 1 documents differed, and the ROI analysis needs every revenue source.
  • ImpactNo cost change. The ROI analysis counts both streams.
  • Approved byProject Manager

CL-03 · Release 1 scope prioritized

  • What changedBacklog ranked with MoSCoW. "Find people to hang out" is Won't have and bus prices are Could have.
  • WhySprint 1 flagged scope growth as a risk. An explicit exclusion list protects the scope.
  • ImpactNo added effort. Keeps the product a planning tool, not a booking platform.
  • Approved byProject Manager

CL-04 · Data-source risk quantified

  • What changedSprint 1's main chain break (dependence on outside data) is now one of three priced risks with expected monetary value.
  • WhyThe budget needs a contingency reserve tied to named risks.
  • Impact$49,750 contingency and a $6,485.58 management reserve are included in the budget request.
  • Approved byProject Manager
