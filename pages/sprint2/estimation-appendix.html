Sprint 2 — Team 11

Estimation Appendix

Two independent estimation methods for the Finding Events and Budgeting slice.

01 Estimated Slice

Finding Events and Budgeting. The slice was chosen because budgeting was strongly identified in interviews and event discovery influences where users travel and what they do.

02 Scope Boundary

The estimates cover the Finding Events and Budgeting slice. The slice name alone does not define its full scope boundary.

03 Estimation Methods

Method 1 — Function Points: starts with the slice’s functional size.

Method 2 — Analogous Estimation: starts with a comparable product and adjusts its effort for the slice and team size. The methods use different starting information.
