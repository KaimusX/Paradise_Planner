02 Business Need

The Sprint 1 research describes fragmented travel planning and budgeting. Travelers compare information across tools and need to understand how destinations, activities, and costs fit together.

See the Sprint 1 Market Research.

03 Proposed Project

Paradise Planner combines itinerary organization with budget awareness. The Sprint 1 business model uses a $1.99 app purchase and affiliate commissions from external travel providers.

See the Business Strategy and Project Charter.

04 Evidence from Sprint 1

Budgeting was strongly identified in interviews. Event discovery influences where users travel and what they do. These findings support the selection of Finding Events and Budgeting for estimation.

09 Team-Authored Go/No-Go Recommendation

Decision: Go with Conditions

Justification

We recommend moving forward with the project with some conditions. The project addresses the travel-planning problem identified in Sprint 1 and has the potential to provide useful value to users.

The estimated effort is 331–828 hours, with a planning estimate of approximately 555 hours for a team of four. The initial budget of $136,197.26 is reasonable if the project reaches its expected level of success.

The expected ROI is approximately +12.2%, which supports moving forward. However, the project would need approximately 89,092 paid downloads to break even. This creates some financial risk because download volume and affiliate revenue are difficult to predict.

The main risks, including download volume, affiliate conversion, API dependencies, and operating costs, are manageable but should be monitored closely throughout the project.

Conditions

Before continuing with full development, the team should:

  • Validate that customers are interested in the product.
  • Confirm that the affiliate revenue model is realistic.
  • Confirm the availability and costs of required APIs and partner companies.
  • Keep development within the estimated budget and time frame.
  • Track downloads, revenue, and operating costs.

Final Recommendation

Go with Conditions: The project has potential and a positive expected ROI, but the team should validate the key financial and technical assumptions before proceeding with full development.
