02 Planning Assumptions

The app price is $1.99. Download volumes, affiliate conversion rates, and average affiliate revenue below are planning assumptions, not historical measurements.

Planning scenarios
Scenario Paid downloads Affiliate conversion Average affiliate revenue
Conservative 50,000 3% $8

Upside 200,000 8% $15

03 Calculation Logic

App revenue
paid downloads × $1.99
Affiliate conversions
paid downloads × affiliate conversion rate
Affiliate revenue
affiliate conversions × average affiliate revenue
Total revenue
app revenue + affiliate revenue
Net benefit
total revenue − applicable project costs
ROI
(net benefit / applicable project costs) × 100

06 Sensitivity

  • Download volume: changes app revenue and the number of potential affiliate conversions.
  • Affiliate conversion: changes how many paid downloads generate affiliate revenue.
  • Average affiliate revenue: changes the revenue earned per conversion.
  • Operating cost: changes applicable project costs and therefore net benefit and ROI.

These relationships follow the calculation logic above; no additional sensitivity estimates or team recommendation have been added.
