Two targets, how much failure each one allows, and what we do when we go over.

Error budget for each objective
Trip cost accuracyBackup service availability
IndicatorDifference between the estimated trip cost and the actual trip cost.Availability of backup functionality when a primary third-party travel service or API is unavailable.
TargetAt least 90% of trip estimates are within ±10% of the actual trip cost.Backup functionality is available at least 99% of the time during third-party service outages.
WindowMonthly, using completed trips with available actual-cost data.Monthly.
Who measuresApplication analytics and the development team, by comparing estimated and actual trip costs.Automated API monitoring and routine system checks.

What spends the budgetA trip estimate that differs from the actual trip cost by more than 10%.Any time backup functionality is unavailable while a primary third-party service or API is down.
Policy actionIf more than 10% of estimates miss the target, pause changes to the estimation feature and review the cost-calculation formula and data sources.If backup downtime exceeds the 1% budget, prioritize reliability work, review backup providers, and add or improve fallback options.

How the budgets are calculated

Trip cost accuracy

SLO = 90%

Error budget = 100% − 90% = 10%

Up to 10% of trip estimates may fall outside the ±10% range before the budget is spent.

Backup service availability

SLO = 99%

Error budget = 100% − 99% = 1%

30 days × 24 hours = 720 hours; 1% of 720 hours = 7.2 hours

Backup may be unavailable for up to about 7.2 hours per month before the budget is spent.
