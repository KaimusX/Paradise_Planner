Project charter

Scope boundary, assumptions, and stakeholder register — the anchor document for the rest of the portal.

Business objective

Make travel planning easier when considering budgets and deciding places to go. We plan on using affiliate associations to fund the app. Market research shows cost and events at locations are the most important things on a trip.

Scope boundary

An app that allows the user to properly organize and make trip decisions. Organization is defined as having cost, time, and activities viewable by day. Trip decisions include deciding where to go, where to stay, and what the best flights are.

Constraints

  • There is simply no way it can grab information about every single miniscule event happening.
  • Redirects outside the app will be inevitable.

Assumptions

  • There is event data available.
  • There is flight data available.
  • There is hotel pricing available.
  • We can calculate cost for hotels, gas, flights, food, and events.
  • People have at least the budget for the lowest cost trip.
  • Trips will occur within a 12 month period.

Success criteria

Can someone plan a trip using this more efficiently than manually?

PM authority

PM has full authority to question the scope and suggest adjustments to developers.

Process QA

Eight quality attributes we're tracking this sprint.

Functional stability Completeness, correctness, and appropriateness is only important in the final user-facing product. This would be something introduced in pre-release. Dedicated sprints to optimize the product for stability once we have the idea, and prototypes done.
Performance efficiency This ties in with usability. After performing usability studies, we will determine these metrics and apply changes, likely after the first few prototypes.
Compatibility Compatibility will first be considered during the design and early development stage, once we know which platforms, devices, browsers, APIs, and outside services Paradise Planner will use. At this point, we will identify the environments that the application needs to support and make sure our design does not depend on features that only work on one specific device or platform.
Usability Usability will be introduced during the initial design and prototype stage because Paradise Planner is intended to make travel planning easier than doing it manually. Early prototypes should therefore focus on creating simple navigation and making important information such as cost, dates, activities, transportation, and lodging easy for users to find.
Reliability Reliability should be considered during the initial development and testing stages of the design, with additional improvements that are being made to make the application feel complete. We would like to make sure the trips and their information are saved properly, and can handle problems with the outside information about flights, hotels, etc.
Secure Security will be considered during the initial design and development stages. We will protect user information, keep data secure, and make sure outside services and unauthorized users cannot access this information.
Maintainability Maintainability is crucial to ensuring the product stays up to date, functional, and keeps the user’s data secure. We will be introducing maintainability when the product is finished and have been sent out for users to enjoy.
Portability Portability needs to be introduced in the initial design and prototype stage because every application will run, feel, and react differently on different platforms like laptop, app, mobile browser, and so on. We would need to map out what exactly we want to run our product on, and when we prototype we cross reference into other platforms to adjust accordingly.
