Sprint 1 — Team 11

Business strategy

Paradise Planner turns a fragmented travel-planning process into one organized experience. Our strategy is to help travelers compare destinations, costs, transportation, lodging, and activities while keeping their total budget and daily itinerary visible.

The strategy-to-project chain

From a traveler’s need to the value our project should deliver.

  1. Customer Need

    Travelers often have to search multiple websites to compare destinations, hotels, transportation, activities, and costs.

  2. Business Opportunity

    Create one planning experience that helps users understand the expected total cost of a trip while deciding what they want to do.

  3. Product Strategy

    Combine trip organization, budget awareness, destination comparison, lodging, transportation, and activities into one planning workflow.

  4. Project Capabilities

    Provide daily itineraries, estimated trip costs, hotel and transportation options, activities/events, and links to external booking providers.

  5. Business Outcome

    Make trip planning faster and easier while creating opportunities for affiliate-based revenue through relevant travel services.

Business objective

Paradise Planner is designed to make travel planning easier when users are balancing cost, destination choices, transportation, lodging, and activities. Instead of requiring travelers to manually collect information from several websites, the application brings the most important planning information into one organized experience.

Goal: Help users create a realistic trip plan faster than they could using a manual planning process.

Value proposition

  • Budget awareness

    Users can see major trip expenses together, including transportation, lodging, food estimates, and activities.

  • Better trip decisions

    Users can compare where to go, where to stay, how to travel, and what to do based on their priorities.

  • Organized itinerary

    Trip activities, costs, and times are organized by day so users can understand the complete trip.

  • Less fragmented planning

    Paradise Planner reduces the need to manually manage information across many unrelated travel websites.

Revenue strategy

Paradise Planner is intended to use affiliate partnerships as its primary revenue model. When a user chooses to continue with a hotel, flight, event, or other travel-related service, Paradise Planner may redirect the user to an external provider. Affiliate relationships can allow the application to generate revenue without requiring users to pay simply to create and organize a trip.

External redirects are an expected part of the product because Paradise Planner is a planning tool rather than the direct provider of every travel service.

Strategic priorities

Our research indicates that cost and available activities/events are two of the most important factors people consider when planning trips.

  1. Cost visibility

    Make the financial impact of trip decisions easy to understand.

  2. Useful activities

    Surface meaningful events and activities without attempting to capture every possible local event.

  3. Simple organization

    Keep dates, times, activities, transportation, and expenses understandable in a day-by-day format.

  4. Efficient decision-making

    Reduce the number of separate searches and tools a traveler needs to create a usable trip plan.

How we will know the strategy is working

The primary test is whether users can plan and organize a trip more efficiently than by manually searching multiple websites and organizing everything themselves.

Trip planning completion
Can users create a complete trip using Paradise Planner?
Planning efficiency
Can users finish planning faster than when using multiple websites manually?
Budget visibility
Can users understand the estimated total cost of their trip?
Decision confidence
Can users compare destination, transportation, hotel, and activity choices without becoming confused?
Usability
Can users complete the main planning workflow with minimal assistance?

From strategy to scope

Strategic goal

Make travel planning easier, more organized, and more budget-aware.

What the product must therefore support

  • Destination decisions
  • Hotel/lodging comparison
  • Flight or transportation comparison
  • Estimated trip costs
  • Activities and events
  • Daily itinerary organization
  • External booking/service links

Data dependencies and constraints

We rely on external sources for event, flight, and hotel information. The app cannot realistically include every small or local event, and some actions require redirects to external providers.

Planning assumptions

We assume usable event data, flight information, and hotel pricing are available; costs can be estimated for hotels, gas, flights, food, and activities/events; and trips are planned within approximately 12 months.

Features that do not directly support these goals should be questioned before being added to the project scope.
